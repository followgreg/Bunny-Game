Privacy Policy

The Bunny Game Arcade has no accounts, no logins, and no sign-up. It does not ask you for your name, email address, or any other personal information, and there is no server storing your gameplay.

What is stored on your device

Games save progress — levels reached, high scores, daily puzzle results, and preferences — in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device, is not transmitted to us, and is not readable by any other website. Daily puzzle games automatically delete entries older than the current day. Clearing your browser storage for this site erases all of it permanently.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand which games get played and how people move around the arcade. It records aggregate information such as pages viewed, approximate region, browser and device type, and referring site. It is used to decide what to build next, and nothing more. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Advertising

This site displays advertising through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party cookie use for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.

Third-party content

Some games load public content at runtime — for example artwork metadata from museum open-access APIs, or public-domain literary texts from Project Gutenberg. These requests are made by your browser directly to those services, which may log the request as they would any web request. No information about you is attached to them.

Children

The games here are suitable for all ages, but the site is not directed at children under 13 and knowingly collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.
