Support — Grrroady Eye

Need help with Grrroady Eye? You're in the right place.

How to Play

Tap and hold anywhere on the screen to fly up. Let go to fall. Steer the eye through the gap between each pair of poles. Touching a pole ends the run. Your score is the number of seconds you survive, and your best time is shown on the title screen.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it supposed to be this hard?

Yes. The gap between the poles shrinks every second and starts moving up and down. By around the 40-second mark it's barely taller than the eye. Lasting a full minute is a real achievement — most runs end well before that, and that's by design.

Do I need an account to play?

No. Grrroady Eye works immediately with no sign-in and no account. The only time it uses the internet is to load the banner ad at the bottom of the screen.

Is there anything to buy?

No. Grrroady Eye is completely free with no in-app purchases. It's supported by a single banner ad — there are no full-screen ads, no video ads, and nothing to unlock with money.

How do I turn off sound or music?

Tap the gear icon in the top corner of the title screen or the results screen to toggle Sound and Music. The gear is hidden during a run so it can't be tapped by accident.

Will I lose my best time if I close the app?

Your best time is saved on your device and will be there the next time you open the app. It is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app clears it.

Can I share my score?

Yes. On the results screen, tap Share to open your device's share sheet with a short line of text about your run. We don't see or store what you share.

Ads and privacy

Ads are served by Google AdMob. In regions with specific consent requirements, such as the European Economic Area and the UK, ads are shown on a non-personalized basis. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.
