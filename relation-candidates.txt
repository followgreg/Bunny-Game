CONSTRAINT USED
  The brief gives two incompatible rules: "no two words share a letter",
  and "ideally no letter appears more than twice". The brief's own
  examples follow the second, not the first:
    FOG/HAIL/SLEET/SHOWER  is labelled "Duplicate letters: None" but runs
                           O2 H2 L2 S2 E3 - it breaks even the looser rule
    HAY/BARN/FENCE/TROUGH  is labelled "None" but has five letters twice
  So the rule enforced here is: NO LETTER MORE THAN TWICE across the 18
  tiles. A set reaching 18 entirely distinct letters is marked
  ZERO-DUPLICATE and is the strongest candidate for a clean grid.

  Grid path solvability is NOT checked here, as instructed.

  Duplicate tiles needed = the count of letters listed on the Duplicate
  line. A set with 3 duplicated letters needs 3 repeated tiles in the grid.
