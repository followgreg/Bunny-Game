Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to play?

No. Jiggle Monster works immediately with no sign-in, no account, and no internet connection required.

Is there anything to buy?

No. Jiggle Monster is completely free, with no in-app purchases and no ads.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?

Tap the music-note icon in the top corner of the game screen to adjust sound and vibration settings.

Will I lose my progress if I close the app?

No — your board, score, and level are saved automatically and will be right where you left them the next time you open the app.

How does scoring work?

Clearing a shape scores points based on its size, multiplied by how many shapes you've cleared back-to-back. Clearing quickly earns an extra speed bonus. Your best-ever score is shown under the score during play.

What's Puzzle Mode?

Every 10th level is a special challenge: match a preset pattern shown on the shelf within 5 moves to earn a large bonus. Every Puzzle Mode board is guaranteed solvable. Missing it doesn't end your game — you just move on to the next level without the bonus.

Still stuck, or found a bug?

We'll get back to you. Please include your device model and iOS version if you're reporting a bug — it helps us track it down faster.
