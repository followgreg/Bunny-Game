THE CLUSTER HIT THE WALL

Match 3 to Pop

Fire bubbles at the planet's cluster. When 3 or more of the same colour touch, they explode off into space — and anything they were holding up flies off too. Chain reactions score big.

Bounce Off the Walls

Your shot bounces off the left and right walls. Use wall shots to reach bubbles hidden around the back of the cluster.

New Bubbles Every 5 Shots

Every 5 shots, 3 new bubbles fly in from deep space and join the cluster — all the same colour. Miss a shot completely and 3 extra arrive as a penalty.

5 in a Row Clears Everything

Pop something on 5 shots in a row and the whole board is blasted off into space, plus a huge bonus. Keep the streak alive to earn the wipe.

Clear the Board to Advance

Clear every bubble off the planet for 1000 bonus points and the next board. Each one starts with a bigger cluster. How many can you survive?
