How to Play

The grid starts full of ramp pieces. The marble drops from the top and follows them — ramps bend it diagonally, empty cells let it fall straight through.

Tap any ramp to cycle its state — right ramp ◢, left ramp ◣, or off (marble passes straight through). Figure out which ramps to set and which to clear to guide the marble to the gold target ●. Hit Drop to test your path — no penalty for retrying.
