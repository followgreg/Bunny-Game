Privacy Policy — OK Analog

Effective date: September 26, 2026

OK Analog is built by Never Stop Creating, LLC. This policy explains what the app does and doesn't do with your information — plainly, since being straightforward about this is part of the point of the app itself.

What OK Analog stores, and where

OK Analog stores your session history — when you started and stopped a moment, which activity you chose, and any note you wrote — directly on your device, using Apple's SwiftData framework. Never Stop Creating, LLC does not operate a server, does not have its own database of user data, and cannot see, access, or receive your session history, notes, or any other content you create in the app.

Location

If you choose to use the optional "Check in" feature, the app will ask for your location only while the app is in use — never in the background, never continuously. This is used for a single, one-time lookup of nearby points of interest (such as libraries, parks, museums, or cafés) so you can optionally attach a place to that session. The lookup is performed by Apple's Maps service, which receives your approximate location in order to return nearby places, under Apple's own privacy practices. If you confirm a place, its name and coordinates are saved with that session on your device — the same way the rest of your session history is stored — and stay there until you delete that session. This data is not otherwise sent to Never Stop Creating, LLC or any third party. This feature is entirely optional and skipping it has no effect on how the app works.

Purchases

OK Analog offers an optional unlock (a monthly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase) handled entirely through Apple's StoreKit and the App Store. Never Stop Creating, LLC does not collect, see, or store your payment information — that is handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy practices.

What OK Analog does not do

OK Analog contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software of any kind. It does not create a user account, does not require sign-in, and does not share, sell, or transmit your data to any third party. It never silences notifications, engages Focus/Do Not Disturb, or otherwise interferes with your phone's ability to reach you.

Children's privacy

OK Analog is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same page with a revised effective date.
