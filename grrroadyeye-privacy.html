Privacy Policy — Grrroady Eye

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Never Stop Creating, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Grrroady Eye mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you play.

Information We Collect

Grrroady Eye does not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or any other information that identifies you personally.

The only gameplay data the App keeps is your best score, which is stored locally on your device. It is never transmitted to us, and we never see it.

Third-Party Advertising

Grrroady Eye is supported by advertising through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device advertising identifiers and other non-personal information to serve ads, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

For players in regions with specific consent requirements — such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom — ads may be served on a non-personalized basis, and any applicable regional requirements are handled by Google AdMob as part of serving ads.

We do not otherwise use analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, or any service that profiles you across other apps or websites.

Sharing Your Score

If you tap the Share button after a run, your device's native share sheet opens with a short line of text about your score. This uses your phone's built-in sharing — we don't see or store what you share, or who you share it with.

Children's Privacy

Grrroady Eye does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the address below and we will address it.

Data Security

Because we don't collect or store personal information on our servers, there's no personal account data of yours for us to lose, leak, or have breached. Your local high score lives only on your device and is removed if you uninstall the App.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
